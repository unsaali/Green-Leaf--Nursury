About GreenLeaf

We are plant lovers on a mission to bring the beauty of nature into every Pakistani home.

Our Story

GreenLeaf Nursery was started in 2018 by a small family of plant enthusiasts in Lahore. What began as a passion project in our backyard has grown into one of Pakistan's most trusted online plant stores.

We believe that plants are not just decoration - they purify the air, reduce stress, and bring life and colour into any space. Our goal is to make high-quality, healthy plants accessible to everyone across Pakistan.

Today, we deliver to over 20 cities and have helped thousands of families create their own little green paradise at home.
